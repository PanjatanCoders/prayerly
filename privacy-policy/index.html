Prayerly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Prayerly ("the app") is developed by Mohammad Saddam Hossain / Raza Technology Solutions. This page explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what it does not do with it.

Summary: Prayerly does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any server. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising in the app. Location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction, and all app data stays on your device.

Information the app accesses

  • Approximate location (coarse): used to calculate prayer times for your area and to determine the Qibla direction. The app does not request or use precise/fine location or background location.
  • Device compass/magnetometer: used only while the Qibla compass screen is open, to point toward Mecca.

Location and compass data are processed entirely on your device to run the app's features. Prayerly does not have a backend server, so this information is never uploaded, stored remotely, or shared with us or any third party.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, prayer preferences, Qaza tracking counts, and Tasbih (dhikr) counter progress are saved locally on your device only, so they persist between app launches. Uninstalling the app removes this data. Nothing is backed up to a cloud account operated by us.

Permissions the app requests

PermissionPurpose

Third-party services

Prayerly does not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service. The only external lookup the app performs is a device-level geocoding request (via your device's operating system) to translate coordinates into a readable place name — this does not go through servers operated by us.

Children's privacy

Prayerly does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no account system and no way for users to submit personal information to us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
